# Engineering Knowledge Base
## RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines retrieval with language generation. A retriever finds relevant evidence and the generator uses that evidence to formulate a response.
## Chunking
Chunking breaks long documents into smaller passages so retrieval can identify focused evidence. Overlap helps preserve context across chunk boundaries.
## Evaluation
Useful retrieval metrics include Recall@K, Precision@K and Mean Reciprocal Rank. Generation can be evaluated for groundedness and answer relevance.
